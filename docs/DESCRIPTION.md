# Projektbeschreibung - Bartresen Küche

## Design Brief: Solid-Wood Standing-Height Worktop with Wall-Side Shelf Unit and Open Bar Extension

### 1. General concept
A continuous solid-wood worktop at one single working height, running in a straight line for 160–170 cm. It transitions from a closed-storage section (against a wall) into an open bar section (past the end of the wall) without any perpendicular leg or L-shape. A separate narrow shelving unit sits on top of the worktop against the wall.

### 2. Top-view geometry (critical — do not reinterpret as an L-shape)
- The front edge (user/room side) is one continuous straight line, full 160–170 cm length.
- **Section 1 ("wall section")**: 120 cm long × 50 cm deep. Its back edge sits directly against the wall.
- The wall ends after these 120 cm.
- **Section 2 ("bar section")**: continues in the same direction for 40–50 cm. It does NOT turn 90°. Instead, only the back edge steps back by 20 cm (into the space vacated by the wall ending), making Section 2 70 cm deep while the front edge stays perfectly aligned with Section 1.
- Total footprint: 120×50 cm followed by 40–50×70 cm, total length 160–170 cm.
- This step lets the worktop visually wrap around the point where the wall ends.

### 3. Worktop height (single continuous plane)
- Both Section 1 and Section 2 are the SAME worktop, at the SAME height — there is no step, notch, or level change in the top surface itself.
- Target height above finished floor: **106 cm** (acceptable range 104–108 cm).
- Height is deliberately taller than a standard kitchen counter (~90 cm) because the primary user is 198 cm tall (secondary user 174 cm). Do not default to standard kitchen height.
- Thickness: 30–40 mm (target 40 mm).

### 4. Substructure — this is where the two sections differ (not the top surface)

**Section 1 (under the 120×50 cm area):**
- Fully closed, floor-to-underside-of-worktop storage block.
- Two ~60 cm wide drawer modules side by side, each with 3 drawers (shallow / medium / deep).
- This block also structurally supports the worktop in Section 1.

**Section 2 (under the 40–50×70 cm area):**
- Fully open — no cabinet, no drawers, no shelving underneath.
- Supported only by 2–3 slim legs/feet (posts), positioned so they do not block knee room for a seated person.
- Purpose: legroom for one bar stool tucked into this open zone, seated with their back roughly toward where the wall used to end.
- Stool target seat height: 74–76 cm (≈30–32 cm clearance under the 106 cm worktop).

### 5. Cutting board
- Separate, loose (not built-in), solid wood, approx. 40–50 cm × 35–45 cm × 30–40 mm, placed on Section 1 when in use.

### 6. Wall-side shelf unit (stands on top of the worktop, Section 1 only)
- A narrow, freestanding open shelving unit that sits **ON TOP of the worktop surface**, positioned along the back/wall edge of Section 1 (not wall-mounted, not floor-standing — it rests on the countertop itself).
- The unit stands on short feet/risers ("Stelzen") that lift its base slightly above the worktop surface.
- Depth: 15–20 cm (target 18 cm) — must stay shallow so it doesn't intrude into the working area.
- Shelf board heights, measured from the worktop surface (not the floor):
  - Lowest shelf board: **+50 cm** above the worktop.
  - Highest shelf board: **+100 cm** above the worktop.
  - (An optional middle shelf between these two heights may be added if it looks balanced — final count is 2 or 3 boards.)
- Open construction (no back panel, no sides) — visually light, for spices, glasses, small bowls, cookbooks, etc.
- Only spans the depth/width of Section 1 (120 cm), since Section 2 must remain open above for the bar/stool area.

### 7. Electrical
- 2× Schuko outlets (optionally USB-C), concealed, positioned at the rear of Section 1 (e.g., below the lowest shelf board or at the worktop's back edge), wiring hidden inside the structure.

### 8. Material
- Solid wood (species and finish not yet decided — treat as a placeholder solid timber for now; do not spend design effort on material/finish detail).

### 9. Required output drawings
- **Top view**: showing the wall (120 cm only), the straight continuous front edge, the 20 cm back-step at the wall's end, drawer block footprint, the 2–3 support feet in Section 2, stool position, and the shelf unit footprint along the wall edge of Section 1.
- **Front elevation**: 106 cm worktop height, drawer fronts, open bar leg space, stool, and the shelf unit with its two (or three) boards at +50 cm and +100 cm above the worktop.
- **Side elevation**: showing worktop depth changing from 50 cm to 70 cm, shelf depth (15–20 cm) sitting on top of the worktop on its short risers, and stool seat height relative to worktop height.
- **3D perspective**: clearly showing one continuous worktop height/plane across both sections, the back-step geometry (not a perpendicular leg), the open leg space with visible support feet in Section 2, and the shelf unit standing on the worktop against the wall.

### 10. Do-not list
- Do not turn Section 2 into a perpendicular L-leg.
- Do not give Section 1 and Section 2 different worktop heights — it is one continuous plane.
- Do not enclose Section 2 with cabinetry, doors, or shelving underneath — only the 2–3 support feet.
- Do not wall-mount the shelf unit — it stands on the worktop on its own short risers.
- Do not extend the shelf unit over Section 2.
